About this prototype

  • Target: LN_IC50, the natural log of the drug concentration that inhibits 50% of cell growth. Lower means more sensitive.
  • Inputs: tissue, MSI status, growth type, screen medium and cancer type (typical for the chosen tissue), plus drug, target, pathway and max tested concentration.
  • 80/20 train-test split, grouped by cell line. Metric: Mean Squared Error.
  • Limitation: the data has no per-cell-line gene expression, so predictions describe a tissue-level profile, not an individual line.
  • Cell lines are laboratory models. These outputs are research estimates, not treatment advice.
